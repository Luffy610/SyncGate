## Caveats

- LFW is web photos of public figures, not factory gates. It is a standard, public check of the model and pipeline, **not** a substitute for the consented field set in ADR-0020.
- Faces are synthetic placements of LFW crops in gate-sized frames; real pose, light and lens conditions will be harder (and same-day enrolment easier) than here.
- LFW genuine pairs are often years apart; gate probes are days from enrolment, so field FNIR should be lower than the per-comparison FRR above.
- No liveness (PAD) numbers: LFW has no attacks. See ADR-0007.
